Website planning document

Port Harcourt Chamber of Commerce

Connecting enterprise with opportunity.

01

Foundation

Site Name

Port Harcourt Chamber of Commerce

The name identifies the city the organization serves and makes the site's purpose immediately clear to local businesses, residents, and prospective investors.

02

Direction

Site Purpose

The site will be a trusted resource hub for businesses in Port Harcourt and the surrounding Rivers State region. It will promote member organizations, share local events and training opportunities, explain membership benefits, and connect entrepreneurs with useful business information.

Its broader purpose is to strengthen the city's business community by improving visibility, encouraging collaboration, and supporting growth across energy, maritime, technology, retail, agriculture, hospitality, and professional services.

03

Audience

Target Market

Business owners, entrepreneurs, corporate leaders, professionals, investors, residents, and visitors in Port Harcourt and across Rivers State.

04

Outcomes

Site Goals

  • Increase participation in chamber programs, workshops, and networking events.
  • Attract new members and make membership benefits easy to understand.
  • Improve the visibility of local businesses through an accessible directory.
  • Present Port Harcourt as a strong destination for investment and enterprise.

06

User journeys

Scenarios

  1. 01

    What membership level best fits my organization, what benefits will I receive, and how do I apply?

  2. 02

    Which business workshops and networking events are happening in Port Harcourt this month?

  3. 03

    Where can I find a trusted local supplier, consultant, restaurant, or professional service?

  4. 04

    What makes Port Harcourt a promising place to open or expand a business?

07

Discovery

SEO Plan

  • Use clear page titles and descriptions containing relevant Port Harcourt business terms.
  • Publish useful event, membership, and community information in semantic HTML.
  • Use descriptive headings, link text, image file names, and alternative text.
  • Encourage member businesses and community partners to link to the chamber site.
  • Keep directory contact details accurate and provide consistent organization information.
  • Measure traffic and common user journeys with privacy-conscious analytics.
